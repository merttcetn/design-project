03State of the Art

Four research families.

Indoor navigation sits at the intersection of four streams. We borrow from each and unify them around natural-language output.

Topological Indoor Maps
Hand-curated node-and-edge graphs of rooms, corridors, and vertical links across floors.
Goetz & Zipf · IndoorGML
Accessibility Routing
Elevators, ramps, stair-free routes for limited-mobility users.
Kumar et al. · Ng et al.
Algorithmic Core
Dijkstra and A* on weighted indoor graphs — deterministic, auditable.
Saha & Banerjee · Google Maps
LLMs for Spatial Reasoning
Turn lists become natural prose; constrained decoding keeps facts grounded.
Jiang et al. · Yamada et al. · Mansourian et al.
OUR GAP
Integrate all four into one system grounded in a real hospital.

06Routing

Dijkstra finds it. The LLM tells it.

Pathfinding
Dijkstra on a small, weighted graph.
weight(edge) = base_cost if avoid_stairs and edge.kind == 'stairs': weight += 10^9 # near-infinite soft penalty
  • Graph is small (≤ 300 nodes); A* heuristic gains nothing.
  • Deterministic — same input, same path. Easy to audit.
  • Accessibility weights compose without re-architecting.
Instruction Rewriting
MiniMax-M2.7 with strict JSON schema.
  1. INraw turn list — e.g. KAT_1_10 → 19_1 (asansör) → KAT_2_07
  2. SCHEMAJSON schema with enum landmarks; no free fields
  3. CLEANstrip <think>…</think> traces before parsing
  4. OUTfluent Turkish, landmark-anchored, accessibility-aware
  5. FALLif schema fails → deterministic templated prose

11What's Next

Beyond one hospital.

The architecture generalizes to any indoor environment with a graph and a story to tell.

Immediate Impact
  • Self-service wayfinding reduces reception load.
  • Accessibility toggle is one switch, not a separate flow.
  • Turkish-first instructions match how patients actually ask.
Next Steps
  • Admin panel: edit doctor rooms & graph nodes live.
  • STT/NLU front-end: speak your destination.
  • AR overlay + visual positioning (anchors).
  • Real-time disruption signals (closures, queues).
  • Formal user study against signage baseline.
